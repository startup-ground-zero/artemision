Our Philosophy

Treatments

Discover a restorative wellness experience designed to support recovery, rejuvenation, and long-term wellbeing. Our carefully curated thermal spa treatments combine the healing benefits of natural thermal water with personalised wellness guidance to help restore balance, vitality, and inner harmony.

From rejuvenating anti-ageing therapies and aesthetic treatments to tailored nutrition guidance, pool therapy, and specialised treatments for the body and face, each experience is thoughtfully designed to meet your individual needs and support a more holistic approach to health.

Reconnect with your body, restore from within, and experience the therapeutic benefits of treatments designed to promote relaxation, recovery, and overall wellbeing.

Therapeutic Massage

A deeply restorative treatment combining skilled therapeutic pressure, relaxation techniques and rhythmic movement. Tailored to each guest's individual needs — whether you seek relief from muscle tension, improved circulation or simply a profound state of calm. Available as a full-body or targeted session.

Hydrotherapy

The therapeutic use of water in varied temperatures and pressures to stimulate circulation, ease joint and muscle discomfort and support the body's natural healing response. Our hydrotherapy treatments draw on the unique mineral richness of Edipsos' thermal waters for amplified restorative benefit.

Salt Therapy — The Power of Minerals

Discover the beneficial properties of halotherapy in a space designed to recreate the therapeutic microclimate of natural salt caves. A completely natural method that strengthens the body through the inhalation of trace elements and skin contact with salt.

Key benefits:

— Respiratory protection
— Antibacterial & anti-inflammatory action
— Improvement of skin conditions
— Rich in trace minerals
— Mental calm

A salt therapy session is the finest gift for detoxifying your body and strengthening your natural defences.

Head Spa

A deeply restorative scalp and head ritual combining warm oil application, targeted acupressure and gentle massage techniques. Designed to release the tension held in the scalp, neck and shoulders, restore mental clarity and guide you into a state of profound, unhurried calm.

Artemision Spa

Presotherapy — Lymphatic Massage

Presotherapy is a painless, non-invasive method that uses air pressure to activate lymphatic circulation and improve blood flow. The ideal solution for those seeking detoxification, body contouring and relief from fatigue.

Key benefits:

— Lymphatic drainage & detoxification
— Reduction of cellulite
— Relief from swelling
— Improved circulation
— Post-exercise recovery

A single session delivers an immediate feeling of lightness, rejuvenation and lasting wellbeing.

The Pool

Thermal Spa Pool:
The Healing Power of Water

Immerse yourself in the ultimate experience of relaxation and healing in our 146 sq.m. thermal spa pool. Combining the unique properties of natural thermal water with modern hydrotherapy systems, we offer targeted care for every part of your body.

Key benefits:

  • specialised neck massage: Dedicated water jets (cascades) release thermal water under pressure, providing immediate relief from tension, stiffness and pain in the neck and shoulder area.
  • targeted lower back hydrotherapy: Side jets focus on the lumbar region, helping to relax the muscles and decompress the spine.
  • foot reflexology: Dedicated devices on the pool floor offer a revitalising massage for the soles of the feet, improving blood circulation and bringing a sense of full-body rest.
  • thermal healing action: The mineral-rich water penetrates the pores, delivering anti-inflammatory benefits and supporting the recovery of joints and muscles.
  • decompression & wellbeing: The buoyancy of the water combined with warmth and bubbles reduces body weight and joint pressure, relieving accumulated stress.

Surrender to the flow of the water and let our specialised hydrotherapy systems restore balance to your body.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who We Are

Artemision Hotel & Spa ("we", "us", "our") is located at Filellinon 5, Loutra Edipsou, 34300, Evia, Greece. We are committed to protecting the personal information of our guests and website visitors in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Greek law.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal data: name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment details (processed securely via third-party providers), and stay preferences. We also collect standard website usage data (cookies, IP addresses, browser type) through our analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to process reservations, communicate booking confirmations, personalise your stay, respond to enquiries, send newsletters (only with your consent), and meet our legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with booking platforms (e.g. Booking.com), payment processors, and service providers strictly necessary for your reservation. All third parties are bound by appropriate data protection agreements.

5. Data Retention

Reservation and guest records are retained for up to 5 years as required by Greek fiscal and hospitality regulations. Marketing data is retained until you withdraw consent.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise your rights, please contact us at info@Artemision.gr.

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and, with your consent, analytics cookies to improve your experience. You may adjust cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at info@Artemision.gr or by post at the address above.
